Whisk 2 minced garlic cloves, 4 anchovy fillets, and 1 egg yolk into a smooth paste.
Squeeze in juice of 1 lemon, then slowly stream 100ml olive oil while whisking.
Stir 50g grated parmesan, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, and a pinch of salt into the dressing.
Toss 2 cups torn romaine leaves with 1 cup cubed toasted sourdough croutons.
Drizzle 4 tbsp of the prepared dressing over the greens and bread cubes.
Add 1 tbsp extra parmesan shavings on top for a sharper, saltier finish.
Chill the assembled salad for 10 minutes so the flavors meld together.
Serve immediately with 2 lemon wedges and a crack of black pepper.
Whisk 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon, and 2 minced anchovy fillets in a bowl.
Slowly drizzle in 1/3 cup olive oil while whisking until dressing thickens.
Crush 1 small garlic clove with 1/2 tsp salt into a smooth paste, then blend in.
Tear 2 heads romaine into bite-size pieces and dry them well in a salad spinner.
Toss the lettuce with 1/2 cup grated Parmesan and the dressing until evenly coated.
Cube 2 cups stale sourdough and toast with 2 tbsp olive oil until golden and crisp.
Add the warm croutons to the salad and toss gently so they stay crunchy.
Finish with cracked black pepper and a final 2 tbsp shaved Parmesan on top.
