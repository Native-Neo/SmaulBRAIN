Whisk 1 cup all-purpose flour with 2 tbsp sugar and 2 tsp baking powder in a large bowl.
Beat 1 large egg with 1 cup milk and 2 tbsp melted butter until smooth.
Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined.
Rest the batter for 5 minutes so the flour hydrates and bubbles form.
Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat and grease it with 1 tsp butter.
Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake onto the hot skillet and spread gently.
Cook each pancake 2 minutes until bubbles pop, then flip and cook 1 minute more.
Stack the finished pancakes and keep them warm under a clean kitchen towel.
Mix 500g bread flour and 350g water, then rest 45 min for the autolyse.
Add 100g active starter and 10g salt, then fold gently to combine.
Perform 4 sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 min apart at room temp.
Bulk ferment the dough 4 to 6 hours until it rises about 70 percent.
Shape the dough into a tight boule and rest it 20 min on the bench.
Place the boule seam-side up in a floured banneton and cold proof 12 hours.
Preheat a Dutch oven to 250C and score the loaf with a single slash.
Bake covered 20 min, then uncovered 25 min at 230C until deep brown.
Dice 2 eggplants, salt them, and rest 20 minutes to draw out excess moisture.
Heat 4 tbsp olive oil in a pot and soften 2 sliced onions for 8 minutes.
Add 3 minced garlic cloves and cook 1 minute until fragrant but not browned.
Stir in 2 diced red peppers and cook 5 minutes until slightly soft.
Fold in 4 diced zucchini and cook another 5 minutes over medium heat.
Add 6 chopped tomatoes, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tsp herbes de Provence.
Simmer uncovered 25 minutes, stirring gently, until vegetables are tender.
Finish with 2 tbsp torn basil and 1 tbsp olive oil, then rest 10 minutes.
Dissolve 2 tbsp white miso paste in 4 cups warm dashi stock, never boiling.
Add 150 g cubed silken tofu and 2 tbsp dried wakame to the hot broth.
Soak 2 tbsp dried wakame in 1 cup cold water for 5 minutes, then drain well.
Slice 3 green onions thinly and stir 1 tbsp into the soup just before serving.
Simmer 1 cup sliced shiitake mushrooms in 4 cups dashi for 6 minutes.
Whisk 2 tbsp red miso with 1/4 cup hot broth, then pour back into the pot.
Add 1 tsp soy sauce and 1/2 tsp sesame oil to 4 cups miso broth for depth.
Top each bowl with 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds and serve the miso soup hot.
Whisk 1.5 cups flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, and a pinch of salt in a large bowl.
Beat 1 large egg with 1.25 cups milk and 3 tbsp melted butter until fully combined and smooth.
Pour the wet mix into the dry ingredients and stir just until no dry streaks remain, about 15 folds.
Rest the batter uncovered for 10 minutes so the flour hydrates and the pancakes turn out tender.
Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat until a drop of water sizzles and dances.
Ladle 1/4 cup batter per pancake onto the pan, spacing them 2 inches apart so edges stay crisp.
Flip each pancake once tiny bubbles pop across the top and the edges look dry, about 2 minutes.
Stack the finished pancakes on a warm plate and serve with 2 tbsp butter and warm maple syrup.
Mix 500g bread flour with 350g water and rest 30 minutes for autolyse.
Add 100g active starter and 10g salt, then mix until fully combined.
Fold dough every 30 minutes for 3 hours, total of 6 sets.
Bulk ferment at 24C for 5 hours until dough rises by 50 percent.
Shape into a round boule and rest 20 minutes on the bench.
Place dough seam-side up in a floured banneton and cold proof 12 hours.
Preheat Dutch oven to 250C for 45 minutes before baking.
Bake covered 20 minutes, then uncovered 25 minutes at 230C.
Mix 100g active starter with 350g water and 500g bread flour until shaggy.
Rest 30 minutes, then add 10g salt and 25g water, squeezing to combine.
Fold dough every 30 minutes, four times, covering between each set.
Bulk ferment 4 to 6 hours at 24C until dough rises about 60 percent.
Shape into a round, then bench rest 20 minutes before final shaping.
Place in floured banneton, cover, and cold proof 12 to 16 hours at 4C.
Heat Dutch oven to 250C, score loaf, and bake covered for 20 minutes.
Uncover, lower to 230C, and bake 20 minutes until deep brown.
Dice 2 eggplants, 3 zucchini, and 2 bell peppers into half-inch cubes for even cooking.
Heat 4 tbsp olive oil in a large pot and soften 1 diced onion over medium heat for 5 minutes.
Add 3 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp herbes de Provence, stirring for 30 seconds until fragrant.
Stir in the eggplant and zucchini, then cook 8 minutes until they begin to soften and brown.
Pour in 400 g crushed tomatoes, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp black pepper, then mix well.
Simmer uncovered on low for 25 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes to prevent sticking.
Fold in 4 chopped tomatoes and 1 tbsp red wine vinegar during the last 10 minutes of cooking.
Rest the ratatouille off heat for 10 minutes, then drizzle 1 tbsp olive oil before serving.
